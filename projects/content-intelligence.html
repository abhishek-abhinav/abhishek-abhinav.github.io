01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

What this system is designed to do

A content-planning intelligence pipeline designed to recommend what the team should create next by combining historical post performance, audience interests, search trends and real creator content references.

The solution is implemented as an n8n-led workflow using the integrations and logic described below. The focus is to connect the input, processing steps and destination systems into a traceable flow. The reference-content stage discovers actual Instagram Reels, posts or other creator content related to shortlisted topics. These examples act as creative references for the team to study—not as content to copy. The pipeline connects its ranked recommendations to the broader content workflow.

02 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

The problem and objective

A content team needs more than a list of trending keywords. It needs to decide which topics fit its audience and historical performance, understand whether interest is rising, and find real creator examples that can inform the creative approach.

Objective: Build a repeatable workflow that connects the relevant data or event to the appropriate processing logic and records the result in the systems used by the team.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION SCOPE

What I implemented

  • Analyze historical post performance and audience interests
  • process category keywords
  • retrieve weekly trend data for the previous year through SerpAPI and Google Trends
  • batch five keywords per request
  • calculate average trend, recent average, growth rate and trend score
  • score historical content fit and audience relevance
  • combine signals into a weighted opportunity score
  • rank and select the top ten opportunities
  • prepare hooks, scripts, captions, hashtags and CTAs
REFERENCE CONTENT DISCOVERY

From topic opportunity to real-world examples

A defining part of this system is the reference-content stage. Once a topic is shortlisted, the workflow discovers actual Instagram Reels, posts or other creator content related to that topic. These are real examples the team can open and study for presentation style, framing, hooks and creative direction.

The intention is not to copy another creator's work. It is to give the content team relevant examples while deciding how to approach the topic in its own voice.

04 / WORKFLOW ARCHITECTURE

How the workflow operates

The reference-content stage discovers actual Instagram Reels, posts or other creator content related to shortlisted topics. These examples act as creative references for the team to study—not as content to copy. The pipeline connects its ranked recommendations to the broader content workflow.

  1. 01

    Collect the content context

    Bring together historical post performance, audience interests, category definitions and topic keywords.

  2. 02

    Research topic momentum

    Use SerpAPI and Google Trends to collect weekly trend signals. Keywords are processed in batches of five.

  3. 03

    Calculate opportunity signals

    Calculate average trend, recent average, growth rate and trend score, then evaluate historical content fit and audience relevance.

  4. 04

    Rank content opportunities

    Combine the signals into a weighted opportunity score and select the strongest ten topics for consideration.

  5. 05

    Find real creative references

    Discover actual Instagram Reels, posts or other creator content on shortlisted topics so the team can study relevant examples.

  6. 06

    Prepare content directions

    Produce structured recommendations including hooks, scripts, captions, hashtags and CTAs for the wider content workflow.
