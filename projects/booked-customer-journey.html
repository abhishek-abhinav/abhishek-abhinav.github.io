01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Purpose and scope

A post-conversion workflow that moves converted enquiries into a booked-customer record and coordinates pre-trip information, itinerary sharing, reminders and ongoing customer communication.

The workflow begins when an enquiry is marked as booked, moves the record into the booked-customer dataset and uses the travel date and customer record to drive the relevant information and reminder steps.

02 / IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS

What was built

The following items describe the documented scope and workflow activities for this implementation.

  • Move converted customers to the Booked Customer Sheet.
  • send pre-trip information and travel guidelines.
  • share itinerary, required documents and support details.
  • trigger reminders before travel dates.
  • support communication during the travel lifecycle.
  • enable post-booking engagement.
