01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Purpose and scope

A brand-specific lead capture and nurture workflow connecting Meta Lead Ads, Google Sheets and CRM tracking with WhatsApp engagement, qualification, assignment and response tracking.

Lead capture feeds the lead-management dataset. Qualification and routing determine the next WhatsApp message or team assignment. Webhook events update lead and conversation records so the team can track responses and follow-up state.

02 / IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS

What was built

The following items describe the documented scope and workflow activities for this implementation.

  • Capture leads from Meta Lead Ads.
  • maintain lead records in Google Sheets and CRM tracking.
  • automate first WhatsApp engagement.
  • qualify leads and track status.
  • follow up with unresponsive leads.
  • categorize and route customer enquiries.
  • use dynamic WhatsApp templates.
  • assign leads and notify the responsible team.
  • maintain conversation history and response tracking.
  • handle lead updates and customer replies through webhooks.
  • test the end-to-end journey and refine logic.
