01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

What this system is designed to do

A multi-stage lead nurturing system connecting Meta Lead Forms, WhatsApp Business, email and Google Sheets. It adapts follow-ups to customer responses instead of relying only on fixed message blasts.

The solution is implemented as an n8n-led workflow using the integrations and logic described below. The focus is to connect the input, processing steps and destination systems into a traceable flow. The workflow combines scheduled and event-driven paths. A reusable Level 2 sub-workflow can be invoked from the scheduler or the incoming-message webhook. Message IDs are used to update outgoing delivery statuses. Media messages are stored for reference; the chatbot does not attempt to interpret unsupported media.

02 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

The problem and objective

Travel enquiries do not all follow the same path. A useful lead-nurturing system needs to capture the enquiry, communicate across channels, recognize replies, track message status and route high-intent prospects to the team without losing conversation context.

Objective: Build a repeatable workflow that connects the relevant data or event to the appropriate processing logic and records the result in the systems used by the team.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION SCOPE

What I implemented

  • Capture leads from Meta forms
  • maintain a central lead record
  • run Level 1–5 follow-up stages
  • send itinerary and offer messages
  • alert the sales team for hot leads
  • receive inbound WhatsApp messages and delivery-status webhooks
  • match replies to existing leads
  • change the next action based on the customer’s response
  • log incoming/outgoing messages
  • download customer media to Drive
  • and activate a guarded AI chatbot in later stages with conversation history

04 / WORKFLOW ARCHITECTURE

How the workflow operates

The workflow combines scheduled and event-driven paths. A reusable Level 2 sub-workflow can be invoked from the scheduler or the incoming-message webhook. Message IDs are used to update outgoing delivery statuses. Media messages are stored for reference; the chatbot does not attempt to interpret unsupported media.

  1. 01

    Capture the enquiry

    A Meta Instant Form submission enters the workflow with the lead details that the form collects.

  2. 02

    Create or update the lead record

    The incoming details are mapped into the central lead sheet so the team can track the lead and its current nurture stage.

  3. 03

    Start the nurture journey

    The system uses scheduled and event-driven paths to send the configured WhatsApp and email communication across Level 1–5.

  4. 04

    Listen for customer behaviour

    Incoming WhatsApp messages and delivery-status webhooks are received separately, matched to the relevant lead and recorded.

  5. 05

    Choose the next action

    The response can change the next nurture stage or invoke the reusable follow-up sub-workflow. Hot intent can trigger a sales-team alert.

  6. 06

    Maintain conversation context

    Later-stage AI chatbot handling can use conversation history and configured guardrails. Media is stored for reference; unsupported media is not treated as understood.

DETAILED EXECUTION / EVENT LOGIC

How each event changes the customer journey

The workflow uses the lead's current action and next-action fields as the control point. This allows a scheduled nurture run and an incoming WhatsApp reply to coordinate without independently sending conflicting follow-ups.

Level 1: first contact and hot-lead alert

  • When a Meta Lead Form submission is received, the lead is captured in the Lead Master and the configured initial qualification logic is applied.
  • For a hot lead, the sales team receives an alert.
  • The customer receives the Level 1 WhatsApp message asking for the type of travel they are interested in, along with the company profile.
  • The lead record stores the current action and the scheduled next action.

Level 2: immediate response path or 12-hour fallback

  • If the customer replies while Level 1 is the current action, the webhook invokes Level 2 immediately.
  • If no reply arrives, the scheduler sends the itinerary after the revised 12-hour delay from Level 1.
  • Level 2 is implemented as a reusable sub-workflow and can be called from both the scheduled workflow and the incoming-message webhook.

Levels 3–5: scheduled nurture actions

  • Level 3 sends an itinerary follow-up.
  • Level 4 sends an exclusive offer.
  • Level 5 sends an offer-expiry intimation.
  • The scheduler reads the next-action type and time from the lead record to decide which action is due.

Webhook handling: inbound messages and outgoing statuses

  • The webhook handler receives incoming WhatsApp messages and outgoing message-status callbacks.
  • Incoming and outgoing events are written to separate sheets: Incoming Messages and Outgoing Statuses.
  • For a status callback, the message ID identifies the existing status row; the relevant status column is updated on that same row.
  • For an incoming message, the sender's phone number is checked against the Lead Master. Known leads are logged with their lead details and relevant context; unknown senders are appended as new incoming-message records.

Response-driven branching rules

  • Reply during Level 1: trigger Level 2 from the webhook flow immediately rather than waiting for the 12-hour schedule.
  • Reply during Level 2: update the next action to Level 4, revise its time and skip Level 3 for that path.
  • Reply during Level 3 or Level 4: reduce the time until the next scheduled action according to the configured rule.

Media handling and later-stage AI assistant

  • If a customer sends media, the workflow downloads it, saves it to Google Drive and records the Drive link in the sheet for team reference.
  • From Level 3 onward, eligible customer replies can activate the AI chatbot. It responds to questions and attempts to collect required trip information, then updates the lead sheet.
  • The chatbot checks the complete conversation history before responding and logs its AI response for traceability and personalization.
  • The AI does not claim to inspect unsupported media. If a media message has a caption, it can respond using the caption; without a caption, it acknowledges receipt and indicates that the team will get back to the customer.
  • The assistant is bounded: it should collect relevant information, avoid unnecessary conversation and not provide risky or unsupported commitments.
