01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

What this system is designed to do

An automated research workflow intended to compare travel demand and supply signals across destinations and surface potential opportunities.

The solution is implemented as an n8n-led workflow using the integrations and logic described below. The focus is to connect the input, processing steps and destination systems into a traceable flow. The workflow brings destination research signals into a structured process, applies scoring logic and generates strategy notes for further evaluation.

02 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

The problem and objective

Travel opportunity research can involve multiple destinations, source markets and demand/supply signals. The workflow structures this research so opportunities can be compared and reviewed.

Objective: Build a repeatable workflow that connects the relevant data or event to the appropriate processing logic and records the result in the systems used by the team.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION SCOPE

What I implemented

  • Create a structured sheet model
  • configure DataForSEO and SerpAPI
  • rotate destinations
  • discover source locations
  • analyze demand
  • analyze supply and competition
  • calculate opportunity scores
  • generate AI-assisted strategy
  • update the research sheet
  • test and optimize the workflow
