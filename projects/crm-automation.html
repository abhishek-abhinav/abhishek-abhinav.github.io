What this system is designed to do

A collection of connected CRM workflows that reduce manual follow-up administration and improve visibility into sales activity.

Present this as a suite of related workflows rather than one monolithic automation. Each workflow handles a specific operational trigger or reporting need.

02 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

The problem and objective

CRM operations include multiple recurring tasks: follow-up reminders, meeting synchronization, reporting, backup and monitoring. These are easier to manage when handled by focused automations.

Objective: Build a repeatable workflow that connects the relevant data or event to the appropriate processing logic and records the result in the systems used by the team.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION SCOPE

What I implemented

  • Automate due and overdue follow-up reminders
  • report CRM activity
  • sync CRM meetings to Google Calendar
  • monitor missing future follow-up dates
  • prepare daily call-activity reports
  • back up CRM data
  • follow up on closed leads where configured
  • and report referral status
