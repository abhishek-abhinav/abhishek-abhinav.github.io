01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

What this system is designed to do

A reporting and feedback set that connects campaign attribution and lead outcomes with content-level evaluation.

The solution is implemented as an n8n-led workflow using the integrations and logic described below. The focus is to connect the input, processing steps and destination systems into a traceable flow. The reporting flow joins campaign information with downstream lead and booking status. The post-feedback workflow evaluates content performance and summarizes improvement opportunities.

02 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

The problem and objective

Campaign metrics become more useful when they can be considered alongside lead and booking outcomes. Content performance also needs a structured review process rather than isolated post metrics.

Objective: Build a repeatable workflow that connects the relevant data or event to the appropriate processing logic and records the result in the systems used by the team.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION SCOPE

What I implemented

  • Map leads to campaigns
  • aggregate campaign data
  • classify lead and booking statuses
  • calculate reporting measures such as spend, ROAS and profit where source data supports them
  • move unconverted leads into a follow-up sheet
  • track booked customers
  • evaluate published posts within a timeframe
  • produce post-wise scores and a consolidated feedback/action plan

DETAILED IMPLEMENTATION

What the workflow actually does

The reporting flow joins campaign information with downstream lead and booking status. The post-feedback workflow evaluates content performance and summarizes improvement opportunities.

Implementation activities and workflow capabilities

  • Campaign-to-lead attribution.
  • Meta campaign data retrieval.
  • KPI sheet structure.
  • lead status classification including genuine, fake and duplicate.
  • booking-status mapping.
  • spend and booking-based ROAS and profit calculations where source data supports them.
  • unconverted leads after Level 5 moved to Lead Follow-up.
  • booked leads moved to Booked Customer Sheet.
  • current timestamp used if booking date is missing in Inquiry Master.
  • post evaluation within a selected timeframe.
  • post-wise score and verdict.
  • consolidated feedback and action plan.
  • dashboard KPI, CPL, ROAS, CAC, conversion and destination-wise aggregation improvements.
